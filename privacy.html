Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Short version: KEBBEK has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your songs, lyrics, logos and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Songs you add, playlists, lyrics, logos, themes and settings are stored only in the app’s storage on your device (or in your browser for the web version).
  • Songs made with KEBBEK Studio and videos you record are made on your device.
  • Automatic lyrics run on your device. The speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face’s public servers; your audio is not uploaded.

When something leaves your device

  • KEBBEK Cloud (optional). If you choose the cloud engine in Create, your song description, style words, lyrics and, for Cover or Repaint, the selected song are sent to the music engine you picked (the public ACE-Step service on Hugging Face, or a server address you enter). Their own privacy terms apply. If you enter a Hugging Face token, it is sent only to Hugging Face and stored on your device only if you choose “Remember”.
  • Sharing. When you save or share a video, it goes only where you send it.
  • Libraries. The web version loads open-source code (three.js and others) from public code servers (CDNs). These servers can see your IP address, as with any website.

What we do not do

  • No sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no selling or sharing of personal data, no tracking across apps or websites.

Permissions on iPhone

  • Photos and camera: only to pick or take a picture for your logo, and to save videos you make, when you ask.

Children

KEBBEK is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

Delete songs or everything from the Library, or remove the app (or clear the website’s data) to erase all local data.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app changes. Contact details are listed on the KEBBEK App Store page.
